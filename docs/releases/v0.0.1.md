This is the first public release of JustConnect, an aTrust client for Windows and macOS. Connect once, then open course sites in your usual browser or SSH into a lab machine from your terminal. Campus traffic goes through aTrust while ordinary traffic follows your existing network settings.

- **Windows:** Run the installer to install JustConnect for your account. You can choose where it goes and whether to add a Start menu shortcut. If you use the ZIP, extract the entire archive before opening `JustConnect.exe`. Sign-in requires the [WebView2 Runtime](https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/).
- **macOS 14.4 or later:** Open the DMG and drag JustConnect to Applications.

Windows installers are unsigned, and macOS builds are not notarized. Your system may ask you to confirm the first launch.

## Connect

Select the built-in **Fudan University** profile, or add a profile for your own aTrust gateway. Click **Connect** and follow the sign-in and network permission prompts.
